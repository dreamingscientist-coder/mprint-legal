Frequently asked questions

How do rewarded ads work?

Rewarded videos are optional. If a reward does not appear after a completed video, restart the app and try again. Contact support if the issue continues.

Where is my app data stored?

App content, settings, and progress are stored on your device. We do not provide cloud synchronization for this data.

How do I remove local data?

Delete the app from your device. You may also need to remove its data from device-managed backups.
